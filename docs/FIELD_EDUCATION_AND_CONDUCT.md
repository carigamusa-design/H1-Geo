# H1-Geo — Field Education and Participant Conduct
Status: Foundational product requirements; operational policy details require review.

## Teach before earning
The application should teach a participant how to record a useful, accurate observation before allowing field referrals. Education is contextual, multilingual, accessible, and reinforced with short demonstrations and periodic refreshers. The same guidance should be available through H1 continuity teachers.

## Simple field guidance
- Start from places where you are permitted to be; never enter yards, buildings or restricted areas without authorization.
- Do not photograph through windows, track individuals, or collect unnecessary personal details.
- Record only what can actually be observed. 'Fence appears damaged' is not 'owner needs a new fence.'
- Explain when a photograph, voice note or location is useful, optional or unnecessary.
- Distinguish the location of the observer from the location of the observed object.
- If someone declines contact or participation, stop respectfully. No argument, repeated approach or circumvention.
- Do not promise quotes, jobs, eligibility, repairs or compensation before they are authorized and verified.
- Prefer helpful education over volume-driven lead collection.

## Product enforcement
Provide understandable rules before activation; record relevant acknowledgment; detect and review credible violations. Serious or repeated misconduct can suspend field capture, referrals or earning privileges proportionately. Provide notice, evidence review, appeal and restoration paths where appropriate. Do not automatically remove access to someone's personal HomeVault because of a field-program violation.

## Dignity and assistance
Needs-based aid and donated repairs are future H1 service models. Never assume vulnerability, age, income or consent from visual appearance. Let people request help and decide what to share. Any aid decision requires clear eligibility, funding and accountability.

## Engineering acceptance
Tests must prove that denied permissions do not fabricate location; declined contact cannot proceed through an automated outreach workflow; observation status cannot silently become verified; public exports exclude protected metadata. Exact policy implementation is future gated work.

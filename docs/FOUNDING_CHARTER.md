# H1-Geo — Founding Charter
Status: Foundation proposal, GEO-002. Not a claim of deployed capability.

## Mission
Build a durable, globally extensible geographic intelligence foundation for H1 Universe. Help people understand places and physical conditions, locate needs, coordinate useful work, and progressively improve measurement accuracy. H1 is its own first customer: demonstrate real usefulness internally before commercialization.

## Long-term horizon
Shared spatial foundations for homes, construction, fences, roofs, yards, farms, infrastructure, mobility, emergency assistance, drones, sensors, and robotics. The scope is global; implementations must remain jurisdiction-aware and locally validated. A global vision does not imply global coverage today.

## Economic purpose
A location or observation may become an opportunity candidate. Only verified and appropriately authorized needs become actionable work through H1-VNN. Create sustainable revenue and pathways to shared participation, including eventual subsidized or donated services. Do not infer a person's circumstances from a photograph or silently label someone eligible for assistance.

## Non-negotiable engineering doctrine
- Design stable foundations for the whole house; ship the smallest independently useful room.
- Never duplicate authoritative capabilities already owned by H1-SnapNTalk, H1-HomeVault, H1-VNN, H1-Ride-MX, or other H1 domains.
- Geographic location, physical object, observation, and work opportunity are distinct records with explicit links.
- Source, timestamp, uncertainty, coordinate reference system, and provenance are required for defensible measurements.
- Uncertainty is not a defect to hide; insufficient evidence must produce an honest unavailable or unverified state.
- No accuracy, parcel ownership, roof area, fence length, damage, drone readiness, or robotics claim without independent benchmark evidence.
- Vendor-independent interfaces; evaluate provider coverage, quality, costs, data rights, attribution, and failure modes before selecting.
- Education, respect, and proportionate enforcement are product capabilities, not afterthoughts.
- H1 Factory validates isolated experiments before promotion; no automatic promotion to production.
- Keep H1's mission human-centered and avoid hidden extraction, pay-to-rank steering, or unaccountable value distribution.

## Decision rights
Founder authorizes changes in mission scope, new permanent services, integration into consumer systems, external deployment, and paid provider contracts. Evidence gates must be passed, not waived to satisfy a deadline.
